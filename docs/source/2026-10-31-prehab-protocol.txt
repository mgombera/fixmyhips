        PREOPERATIVE REHABILIATION (PREHAB) PROTOCOL FOR HIP
                       ARTHROSCOPY PATIENTS


GOALS

    •    Physical therapy for 2 weeks prior to surgery
    •    Evaluation and establishment of goals after surgery
    •    Advise on maintaining postoperative ROM restrictions (see below)
    •    Instruct on 20# WB with flat-foot pattern with crutches, stair use
    •    Identify if additional equipment needed (e.g. seated toilet, grasper, only on as
         needed basis)
    •    Exercise program for core and gluteal strengthening
    •    Schedule post-op physical therapy visit for 4-7 days after surgery

EXERCISES

    •    Core muscle strengthening, gluteus strengthening
    •    Single leg calf-raises
    •    Single leg squats
    •    Side-lying hip abduction/adduction
    •    Four point kneeling hip rocks
    •    Kneeling hip flexor stretches

POST OP RESTRCTIONS

    •    No sitting with hip flexed >90 degrees to avoid hip flexor irritation
    •    Hip brace when ambulating (typically for 4 weeks, occasionally for 6 weeks)
    •    20lb Weight bearing with foot flat gait pattern (can range from 2 to 6 weeks)
    •    ROM restrictions: Abduction to 45 degrees, no extension past 0, IR to neutral,

FOLLOW-UP

    •    Schedule first post-op therapy visit 4-7 days after surgery
